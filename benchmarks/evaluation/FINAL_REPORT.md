## Results by skill family (all claims below are confounded / not capability evidence)

### dsh-observability — tool access, not reasoning gain

- 22 large generated variants, budget 30: **with 22/22, without 1/22**.
- Large dsh trace (2722 events): with 6/6 twice; without 5/6 twice.
- Frozen dsh holdout (2618 events): with 6/6 twice; without 5/6 twice.
- Compaction trace (2221 events): with 8/8 twice; without 5/8 and 6/8.
- Codex JSONL (5000 events): with 9/9 twice; without 7/9 twice.
- Skill change: exact percentile/tie convention in `references/metrics.md` and `SKILL.md`;
  without the change the with-arm also substituted nearest-rank p95 or an alphabetical
  top-tool tie-break.

### dsh-plugin-security — not replicated (unchanged)

- `large-plugin-audit` (204 files): both arms 8/8.
- `large-plugin-supplychain` (205 files): both arms 7/7; with fewer calls.
- `large-route-authz` training case: with 7/7 twice; without 6/7 twice (missing severity),
  but the independent holdout passed 7/7 at budget 60. The correctness gap is not
  replicated; only a budget difference remains.
- The plugin family is exploratory, not a validated capability claim.

### dsh-runtime-guide — unstable (unchanged)

- `large-runtime-upgrade`: with 6/6 then 5/6; without 5/6 then 3/6.
- Adding an explicit upgrade-refusal protocol did not make the behavior stable across
  trials. One with trial still attempted an in-session global install.
- Treat the skill as guidance, not a hard safety control.

## Framework changes attempted

- case-level `budget.max_tool_calls` is now enforced during the run by the harness, but over-budget trials are now recorded as scored 0/1 so they stay in the intent-to-treat estimate;
- `allow_files` / `allow_prefixes` for declared analysis artifacts, so a helper script or
  staged install directory is not misreported as fixture mutation;
- frozen holdouts with source/prompt/fixture/grader hashes;
- one record store and static site over agent_eval, holdouts and published legacy reports;
- no LLM calls in report generation; no raw prompts, credentials or machine IPs in public
  artifacts.

## Remaining limits

- 22 of the 38 large cases are generated variants of one observability family; they are
  large and discriminating but not 22 independent real-repository domains.
- 25 small/generated cases in the manifest still have no result; they are not part of the
  large-case completion claim.
- Public `benchmarks/results/catalog.json` still links the legacy art-creation bundles; the
  unified local site is the current evaluation surface, not a public publication.
- Results are case-family evidence, not a universal model/skill leaderboard.

## Completion definition — WITHDRAWN

The earlier completion claim is withdrawn. The large-case count and paired coverage were not verified against the records; the sandbox-attribution suite is unsound:

- large-case counts must be re-derived from the record store;
- the three skill families do not have a valid paired attribution result;
- the sandbox prompts and graders are under repair;
- no claim of skill effectiveness is supported.

The 25 remaining small/generated cases are outside the large-case completion set; they are
kept as candidate material, not counted as completed evidence. Public catalog publication
is outside this evaluation scope; the local unified site is the current surface.
