# Procedural Systems

Use this when a controllable construction procedure can clarify intent or generate useful variations.

| Mechanism | Expresses | Useful evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Rules or grammar | Parts, repetition, hierarchy, allowable variation | Component identities, parameters, seeds, rendered candidates |
| Analytic field | Direction, density, deformation, material coordinates over space/time | Field and output correspondence under controlled conditions |
| Constraint system | Valid relationships among generated elements | Constraint outcomes and variants, including intended exceptions |
| Physical simulation | Material response, interaction, persistent dynamics | Initial state, solver/version, time settings, cache/replay and actual render |

Separate semantic parts, construction operations, relationships, and controllable parameters. Expose controls for the current decision and compare alternative algorithms or backends as useful.

Use [algorithmic art](../methods/algorithmic-art/SKILL.md) for rules and fields, [scene 3D](../methods/scene-3d/SKILL.md) for spatial construction, and [web 3D](../methods/web-3d/SKILL.md) for interactive browser inspection. A simulation or mesh may be an offline input to the final medium rather than its runtime.

For editable garment patterns, [GarmentCode](https://github.com/maria-korosteleva/GarmentCode/tree/d449629979028123a5c4dc9e732a2ec19b7fce31) offers composable panels and interfaces. Set dimensions, ease, seams, and material properties for the intended body and use. Its pattern library and simulation setup provide distinct stages. For application backends, consult [Tool capabilities](tool-capabilities.md).
